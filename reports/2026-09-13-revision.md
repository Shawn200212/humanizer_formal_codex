# September 2026 formal revision

This release separates academic editing from everyday website, news and explanatory prose.
The formal edition focuses on understandable scholarly meaning, bounded claims and bilingual consistency.
It is an instruction-and-tool workflow, not a newly trained language model.

## Observed validation

- Skill entry passed the bundled Codex validator in UTF-8 mode.
- 39 relevant helper/core unit tests passed; the corrected helpers also received an independent rerun.
- Six frozen fictional editing cases for this edition: two Chinese, two English, two bilingual.
- A separate executor withheld from the oracle produced the responses; one AI evaluator judged
  factual/semantic fidelity, task/register compliance and one-pass clarity.
- That evaluator found 6 PASS, 0 PARTIAL and 0 FAIL in this edition. Cases with real source ambiguity
  passed by identifying the author decision, not by pretending the copy was ready to publish.
- One nonblocking observation: an additional causal-evidence discussion increased the decision burden in a short editing request. Missing evidence in an excerpt does not establish that the paper's attribution is false.

See [aggregate data](../data/editorial-evaluation-2026-09-13.json).
Raw source passages, responses and judging material remain private; real user draft examples were not uploaded.

## Important limitations

The case author was also the single AI evaluator; there were no human raters. The executor read all
twelve requests, their metadata and both skill instruction sets in one context. This deviated from
the intended case-by-case isolation protocol and is an oracle-withheld batch smoke test, not a
strictly isolated experiment.

The execution requested gpt-6-astra but did not establish an exact backend snapshot or sampling
configuration. There was no old-version or no-skill baseline, repeated sampling or statistical
comparison. These observations cannot quantify an improvement caused by the skill, establish
reader preference, or guarantee generalization or an absence of overfitting.

Editorial instructions remained frozen for this run. A later helper-only regression correction
handled fraction glyphs; the executor neither read nor ran that helper. Helper tests and editorial
behavior therefore have separate evidence records. Surface-token agreement never establishes
semantic equivalence or clear expression.

The earlier abstract corpus and synthetic-filler classifier remain historical artifacts. Their
classification metrics are not measurements of this release's editing quality. All 19 existing
corpus/training Git blobs are retained unchanged under the renamed skill folder.

## Design references

The structure follows the focused, progressively disclosed skill approach described in
[OpenAI's skill documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills).
Research-focus and clear-message guidance is consistent with [Nature Portfolio's author guidance](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/for-authors/write).
These are design references, not external endorsements or universal prose templates.
